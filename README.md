## 👋 Hi, I'm Juan

I'm a **Data Science & Artificial Intelligence** undergrad at **UniSenai** (2nd semester) and a **Data Analyst Intern at [NSC Comunicações](https://www.nsctotal.com.br/)**, the Globo affiliate in Santa Catarina, Brazil.

Still very much a developer in development: I learn by building things that solve my own problems, and lately that means data pipelines and AI agents.

- 🔭 Working on an automated system that evaluates river water samples against the regulatory standards required by **IST Blumenau (SENAI)**. This is my course's applied project, built with Python + PostgreSQL.
- 🌱 Going deeper into LLM agents (Agno, LangChain) and data analysis with Python and SQL.
- 🎯 Looking to grow as a data professional working where data and AI meet.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📚 [agente_traducao](https://github.com/juan-borinelli/agente_traducao)
Multi-agent AI pipeline (Agno + Claude) that translates academic documents of up to ~500 pages and outputs ABNT-formatted DOCX, with a translator agent and an independent reviewer agent.
I built it because I needed to study long technical texts that weren't available in Portuguese.

`Python` · `Agno` · `Claude API` · `PyMuPDF` · `python-docx`

### 📊 [Analyzing Brazil's Transparency Portal](https://github.com/juan-borinelli/analisando-dados-do-portal-da-transparencia) 🚧
Exploratory analysis of 2025 federal employee travel expenses (per diems & airfare) from Brazil's Transparency Portal: which agencies spend the most, monthly patterns, top destinations and outliers.

`Python` · `Pandas` · `Matplotlib` · `Seaborn` · `Jupyter`
